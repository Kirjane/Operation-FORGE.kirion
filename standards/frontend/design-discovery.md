# Design discovery
Read for new screens, redesigns and owner criticism. Sources D1–D3 in [register](provenance.md).

## D1 — Define product experience before pixels (MUST)
Establish primary operator, environment (desk, touch terminal, field, light, noise, network), frequency, time pressure, consequences of error, and top tasks. Walk an actual screen or authorized prototype from entry to outcome; record what is currently rendered versus merely specified. Inventory routes, data/state/permissions and user language. Ask product authority only for consequential unknowns: what must be preserved, role decisions, legal/financial constraints, brand rights and intended reference. A redesign is not permission to remove a workflow. Missing backend capability becomes an explicit dependency, not silent simplification.

Deliver a concise design brief: actor / job / success / existing friction / device / content / brand / approved visual authority / preserved behaviors / exclusions / unresolved decisions. Prioritize by operator frequency and risk, not route count. Example: municipal clerk scans requests and opens dossier; cashier repeatedly adds variants and completes payment; these are different visual problems.

## D2 — Translate references into a design grammar (MUST)
Inspect actual reference(s) at representative widths. Annotate composition, grid, dominant focal point, type roles, spacing rhythm, contrast, surface depth, navigation, information density, interaction and content behavior. Separate what to reproduce (approved visual structure and product affordances) from what to adapt (domain data, legal access, typography licensing, device ergonomics). Reference is not license to copy assets or fake data. If screenshot-only, record unknown interactive states and do not invent authority. Choose a small set of design adjectives with tangible consequences: e.g. civic/quiet = subdued canvas, legible dense registers, restrained accents; hospitality/fast = high-visibility order controls, warm brand, readable totals. A polished marketing reference may be inappropriate for a cash register.

SHOULD compare two materially distinct directions when direction unresolved, using one representative screen and realistic authorized content. Compare tradeoffs in hierarchy, task speed, identity, implementation and responsiveness. Once direction approved, avoid endless speculative options. Build a token/primitive specimen and a representative full screen in actual target; compare side by side at same viewport, role, state and zoom. Record intentional deviations and owner feedback. Do not claim pixel fidelity from class names.

## D3 — Critique as design engineering (SHOULD)
Inspect at thumbnail scale (dominant visual mass), normal scale (reading order and grouping), detail scale (baseline, icons, border, clipping, typography), then interact. Ask: what is the first action? Is there one obvious primary action per task? Does the most important content get space? Is whitespace doing grouping rather than consuming the working viewport? Is the brand visible through shape, typography, color and composition rather than repeated logos? Is this a coherent authored interface or interchangeable card soup?
- Replace unnecessary hero banners on operational screens with compact title/context/action and actionable content. Keep generous pacing and editorial imagery when the job is discovery or marketing.
- Use real task labels, recognizable domain artifacts, meaningful imagery with correct rights. Avoid faux charts, decorative status colors, redundant cards, arbitrary gradients or glass effects unless justified by the visual direction. Use restrained accents to clarify hierarchy, not eliminate creative expression.
- Critique the weakest secondary routes, long labels, empty/error/denied states, not only the hero. For each defect record location, visual symptom, user impact, expected correction and before/after.
- Copy in operator vocabulary: e.g. “Request refund” not “C5 Audited Refunds”; retain necessary domain/technical terms for genuinely technical users. Translate labels, errors, date and number formatting consistently; do not remove legally required warnings or provenance.

Acceptance: trace each major screen to brief; actual role can identify and complete the primary task; same-state screenshots support comparison; key secondary and constrained states receive intentional design; owner feedback resolved or explicitly deferred. Neither subjective praise nor automated contrast alone proves craft.
